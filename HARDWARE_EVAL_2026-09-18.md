## Corrections to earlier conclusions

- The documented BTT TMC5160T Pro with 0.075-ohm sense resistors is specified for approximately 3.1 A RMS; the September 17 note's claimed 2.385 A hardware ceiling is not supported by the manufacturer specification.
- Current depends on GLOBAL_SCALER as well as IRUN. The installed TMCStepper 0.7.3 uses its TMC2160 current implementation for TMC5160 and programs both. IRUN=31 alone does not establish saturation. Software current readback is not a coil-current measurement.
- The motor's exact 3 A rating convention must be established before selecting a matching RMS current.
- The production code requests 350 mA precharge, 2200 mA capture, 1650 mA brake, and 1100 mA taper. Capture transitions to brake at 250 ms.
- No fixed four-second stop is programmed. The profile uses remaining distance and entry speed. Normal stopMove() uses the faster tracking acceleration (up to eight times planned deceleration); inspect actual transition speeds before blaming that path.
- MOTOR_FIGHT indicates measured forward speed below 45% of FastAccelStepper's reported pulse rate for 150 ms after a 150 ms grace period. It does not establish a specific mechanical cause. Its fault path stops pulses and then releases the outputs.
- The encoder is described in the v2 hardware notes as mounted on the motor rear shaft. Confirm mechanically that its motion faithfully tracks the wheel; the sketch header's "wheel shaft" description is inherited and should not be treated as verification.
