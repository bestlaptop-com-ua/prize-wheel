# Party firmware risk audit

Scope: `prize_wheel_gpt/prize_wheel_gpt.ino` at the frozen control baseline
`f5419be`, plus the three sanctioned fixes and the isolated party add-ons on
`codex/party-v1`. This is a source and compile audit. No device, COM port,
motor, encoder, DFPlayer, strip, or phone was touched.

Severity labels follow `PARTY_TASK.md`: **party-ruining**, **visible**, and
**cosmetic**. Likelihood is a relative assessment for one party session, not a
measured probability unless a bench observation is quoted from the task.

## State-by-state AS5600 jump consequence

The historical jump deserves an explicit state audit because a rejected sample
does not have one universal effect:

| State group | Consequence of one non-accumulating implausible frame | Consequence of repeated corruption |
|---|---|---|
| `IDLE_STOPPED` | Position remains at last good count; velocity history is rebuilt, so motion recognition pauses briefly | No automatic fault in idle; a spin can be missed until good samples return |
| `MOTION_CANDIDATE`, `MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT`, `SPIN_PUSH`, `SPIN_RELEASED` | Classification, release, fitting, or reservation waits for `encoderMotionReady()` | After one second without fresh position, existing `FC_ENCODER_STALE` latches and an open spin closes honestly when possible |
| `TARGET_RESERVED` | Precharge waits for velocity to rebuild; target counts are not changed by the bad frame | Position stale faults at 50 ms; velocity wait faults after its bounded timeout |
| `SPEED_MATCH_CAPTURE`, `CONTROLLED_DECEL` | Hardware keeps the last command while velocity rebuilds; no bad angle enters remaining-distance math | Position stale at 50 ms or velocity unusable at 300 ms latches; motor ramps down or emergency-stops only for the existing fight/reversal cases |
| `LANDING_SETTLE` | Stillness window restarts; no success is declared from the bad frame | Position stale latches; no false `CONTROLLED_SAFE` |
| `SOFT_HOLD` | Fade continues; next good sample recovers | A new motion may not be recognized until recovery; the between-spin TMC check is unaffected |
| `FAULT_LATCHED` | Fault remains latched; final spin record may wait for trustworthy stillness | After the existing bounded dead-encoder wait, the record closes as faulted/locked; automatic control never resumes |

## Go/no-go summary

Source inspection and compilation can establish isolation, bounds, and API
use, but not motor naturalness, RF power integrity, RMT/MCPWM coexistence on the
physical board, DFPlayer clone behavior, or the 2 ms hardware maximum. The
delivery is **no-go for guests** until every ordered item in `DELIVERY.md`'s
15-minute bench script passes, especially: no restored/unexpected fault, no NVS
failure, no brownout, `tmc=1`, valid direction calibration, zero add-on timing
overruns, and successful spins in both directions with a phone streaming.
